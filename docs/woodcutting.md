# Ahrivaea Woodcutting

The stonecutter cuts wood the way it cuts stone. Put a log in and pick what you want; no crafting grid, no sticks.
It's only recipes, so it works on a server without players installing anything.

**Wood types:** oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, poplar, crimson and warped
(stems and hyphae), and bamboo blocks.

| Put in | Get |
|---|---|
| A log | its stripped log, its wood, or its stripped wood (1) |
| A wood block or stripped log | stripped wood (1) |
| Any log or wood (stripped or not) | 4 planks, 4 stairs, 8 slabs, 2 fences, 1 fence gate, 2 doors, 1 trapdoor, 2 pressure plates, 4 buttons, 2 signs or 8 sticks |
| Planks | 1 stairs, 2 slabs, 1 button or 2 sticks |
| Block of bamboo (stripped or not) | stripped block (1), 2 planks, 2 stairs, 4 slabs, 2 mosaic, 2 buttons, 1 pressure plate, 1 fence or 1 door |
| Bamboo planks | 1 stairs, 2 slabs, 1 mosaic, 1 button or 2 sticks |
| Bamboo mosaic | 1 mosaic stairs or 2 mosaic slabs |

A log is worth 4 planks, so each recipe gives what those 4 planks would make at a crafting table, rounded down, with
the sticks for free. Stairs and slabs from planks are 1:1 and 1:2, like stone.

The recipes are generated by `tools/woodcutting_recipes.py` (the tables at the top say what makes what); run it again
after changing them.
